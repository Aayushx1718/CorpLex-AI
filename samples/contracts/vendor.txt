SYNTHETIC VENDOR AGREEMENT
Parties: Cedar Demo Labs Ltd (buyer) and Pine Sample Supplies Ltd (vendor).
Effective date: 2026-03-01. Expiration date: 2027-02-28.
1. Supply. Vendor shall provide office supplies under written purchase orders.
2. Payment. Buyer shall pay USD 12000.00 within 30 days after acceptance of the annual supply delivery.
3. Delay. For delivery delayed beyond the agreed date, a credit of USD 100.00 per day applies, capped at USD 1000.00.
4. Renewal. The agreement renews for one year unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before expiration.
