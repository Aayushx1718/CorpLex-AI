# Original synthetic contracts

These four short fixtures were authored for CorpLex AI, contain fictional parties, and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. They are demonstration text, not legal templates or advice. They have no expert risk labels and are not a training dataset. Amounts are fictional contractual terms, not losses.

`expected.json` records independently readable ISO dates and monetary mentions for the four fixtures. Generate PDF/DOCX versions with `backend/.venv/bin/python scripts/generate_samples.py` from the repository root. Eight generated files are placed in the ignored `samples/generated/` directory. All four contract types and both formats are tested against this ground truth. Do not infer PDF pagination from the text files. Generated bytes are demonstration fixtures, not expert annotations or a model training dataset.
